## insilicoPCR 0.15.3

This release reports more of what a PCR would make (products of one primer, of two assays in one tube, across the origin of a circle, and long products from reads). It finds sites it missed on chromosomes, gives the same results run after run, and checks its inputs and output folder more strictly. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/duceppemo/insilicoPCR/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

### Highlights

- **Products of one primer alone**, where a sequence repeats inverted, are reported under their assay and tagged *one primer*.
- **Multiplex reactions**: assays given the same `mix=<name>` in the primer file share a tube, and their primers pair with each other. Such products are reported as `assayA+assayB` and tagged *two assays*.
- **Circular sequences** are searched as circles. These are FASTA records marked `circular=true` or `[topology=circular]`, and contigs of the FASTQ mini-assembly that close on themselves. A product across the origin is reported with its location starting after it ends (`2901-100`).
- **FASTQ: long products.** Products of 1.5 to 5 kb, missed before, are found by baiting the reads in rounds as the reads are extended. Both products of a locus with two sizes (two alleles, a mixture) are found. A product through a tandem repeat is *inconclusive* (`EXTENSION_IN_TANDEM_REPEAT`) instead of a negative.
- **Chromosomes**: a sequence over 1 Mb is searched in pieces. Sites within `-m 2` or `-m 3` that its e-value used to drop are kept, as on a draft of the same genome.
- **Reproducible FASTQ results**: the mini-assembly builds its contigs on one thread, so the same reads give the same contigs and the same report.
- **FASTQ: adapter-rich libraries.** The reads that bait the second pass are trimmed of adapter read-through first: one with an adapter took every adapter dimer of a library (72% of one sample's 23.7 million reads), for the same report.
- **Assemblies broken inside a primer site** are named in *Primers at contig ends*, as breaks between the primers were.
- **Run → Stop** in the window, and the versions of BLAST+, BBTools and Java in `QAlog.txt` and the HTML report.

### Upgrading from 0.15.2

Results can differ from 0.15.2's on the same input:

- New rows: products of one primer, of two assays sharing a `mix=` group, across a circle's origin, and long FASTQ products.
- `AmpliconSize` of a product whose primer site has an insertion or deletion is now both primers in full plus the template between them, instead of the site's span. `GenomeLocation` is unchanged.
- A mismatch in a primer's first `--5p-truncation` bases counts as an unpaired 5' base, not against `-m`.
- Very short products of primers overlapping head to head are reported (they were dropped when BLAST left a primer's 5' bases unaligned).
- Of two probe sites inside a product, equally bound, the row shows the one nearest the product's start.
- FASTQ mode builds the mini-assembly's contigs on one thread, for the same contigs on every run; the other steps were made faster to make up for it. A 9.7-million-read sample took 259 s; a 1.7-million-read sample whose assay took 7 extension rounds, 491 s.

Files and scripts that read them:

- `report.tsv`: a cell a spreadsheet would take for a formula (starting with `=`, `@`, or `+`/`-` without being a number) is written after an apostrophe, `'-ctrl`. Remove it when reading the file with a script. `GenomeLocation` and `ProbeLocation` may start after they end, on a circle.
- `detailed_report/<sample>/<sample>.tsv` holds the hits the rules accept, not every alignment blastn wrote.
- `QAlog.txt` has a `Tools:` line after the settings.

Refused or left out, with a message, where 0.15.2 ran:

- an option given twice (`-t 2 -t 3`);
- an assay of a `mix=` reaction with `+` in its ID;
- an output folder with no report number left (`report(999999).tsv`), before the run's work;
- an output folder holding symbolic links where a run writes;
- a file name with a control or invisible character, names that differ only in case or Unicode form, and a sample name too long for its result files;
- **Open Previous Run** on a run made before 0.15.1 (open its `report.html`, or run it again).

## insilicoPCR 2.0.0

insilicoPCR 2.0.0 judges each primer and probe site by its melting temperature (Tm) on the template. This one rule, the same for every oligo of every assay, replaces the mismatch counts of 1.x. It says why an assay gave no product. It is a major release: the options for mismatch counts are gone, and the licence is now GPL-3.0-or-later. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/duceppemo/insilicoPCR/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

### One Tm rule in place of mismatch counts

A site is accepted when its Tm reaches the annealing temperature, `--anneal-temp` (default 60 °C), less `--tm-margin` (default 5 °C), and when a primer's 3' end passes the 3' rule (`--3p-window`, `--3p-penalty`, as before). The Tm comes from Primer3's thermodynamic alignment (`ntthal`), built in as Java. It is computed on the template under the site, including the bases BLAST left out. `--na`, `--mg`, `--dntp` and `--oligo-conc` describe the reaction; their defaults (50 mM, 3 mM, 0.8 mM, 250 nM) are a typical qPCR mix.

A mismatch count both let through sites that cannot bind and turned away sites that can. On the same data:

| Data | 1.x, `-m 0` (default) | 1.x, mismatches allowed | 2.0 default |
|---|---|---|---|
| 46 *Brucella* genome assemblies, 4-assay qPCR panel | 21 | 59 (`-m 3 --5p-truncation 8`) | 22 |
| *G. pallida* sample, reads (FASTQ mode) | 3 | 1,021 (`-m 1 --5p-truncation 8`) | 3 |

The extra *Brucella* product is one whose probe has a mismatch in a site that still melts above 55 °C. The wiki page [Why Tm](https://github.com/duceppemo/insilicoPCR/wiki/Why-Tm) has the evidence.

### Each assay at its own temperature

`ta=<°C>` on a primer header, written like `size=`, gives an assay its published annealing temperature: `>toxA-F size=312 ta=55`. Its sites are judged at that temperature, and the other assays at `--anneal-temp`, so assays published at different temperatures can run in one file. The assays of one `mix=` reaction share one temperature.

### Why an assay gave no product

A new file, `report_no_product.tsv`, and the HTML report's **Why no product** section list each assay without a product in a sample. Each line names the oligo with no accepted site, its best site and that site's Tm, and the rule the site fails, or says that the sites pair into no product. The log and `QAlog.txt` list each oligo's Tm on a perfect match, and flag an oligo too short or too A/T-rich to anneal anywhere at the run's temperature.

### Reports

`report.tsv` has three more columns after `SizeCheck`: `ForwardTm`, `ReverseTm` and `ProbeTm`. The columns of 1.x keep their places. The HTML amplicon table shows them.

### FASTQ mode

The reads are baited with 1 edit per k-mer. Oligos of 18 to 21 bases then get a second pass, with 2 substitutions per 18-base k-mer: of random sites the Tm rule accepts, it cut the share whose reads were missed from 7% to 1.3% with 2 substitutions, and from 19% to 13% with 3. Oligos too short for the bait's k bait in a pass of their own, instead of bringing every oligo's k down to theirs. On a *Globodera* panel with 15-base probes, the run took 38 minutes instead of 3 hours. [Limitations](https://github.com/duceppemo/insilicoPCR/wiki/Limitations#known-gaps) lists the sites that can still bait no read.

### Upgrading from 1.0.3

- **Removed options.** `-m`/`--mismatches`, `--probe-mismatches` and `--5p-truncation` no longer exist. A command line that gives one stops and says what to use instead: a lower `--anneal-temp` or a higher `--tm-margin`. In the window, **Anneal (°C)** replaces **Mismatches**. Amplicon settings now has the Tm margin and the reaction, and no longer the probe mismatches or the unpaired 5' bases.
- **Different products.** Expect different results from runs that allowed mismatches. A site with mismatches is now kept when it still melts high enough, and a perfect site of a short or A/T-rich oligo can be rejected. For each assay without a product, `report_no_product.tsv` says which. To accept weaker sites, lower `--anneal-temp`, give the assay its own `ta=`, or raise `--tm-margin`.
- **Refused settings.** A `--tm-margin` at or above the annealing temperature is refused, and so is a reaction with no cations for a Tm (`--na 0` with `--mg` at or below `--dntp`).
- **Report columns.** Scripts that read `report.tsv` by column position keep working up to `SizeCheck`. The three Tm columns follow it.

## insilicoPCR 2.1.0

insilicoPCR 2.1.0 makes the synthetic gel say how each band was made, tells a product of the reads' mini-assembly from one of an assembly given as input, and fixes five bugs found in a review of the code. FASTQ runs do less work, and the window no longer stops answering while a large report is read for the gel. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/duceppemo/insilicoPCR/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

### The gel says how each band was made

Hovering over a band shows how each primer and the probe bind: their mismatches and indels, those in the 3' window, their unpaired 5' bases and their Tm. It also shows the band's weakest site and what the product was called on: an assembly, the reads' mini-assembly, or how many reads support it. Co-migrating amplicons each give their weakest site, and **Band details** adds each one's binding, contig, location and strand. A saved SVG keeps the same text as each band's title. The search also finds bands by a primer or probe ID (`GamelRos-R`).

### `mini-assembly`

For FASTQ input, insilicoPCR assembles the baited reads with Tadpole and searches the contigs. A product found on them now has `Source` `mini-assembly` in `report.tsv`. Before, it had `assembly`, as a product of an assembly given as input had. The HTML report tags these contigs, and the gel says "Called on: mini-assembly of the baited reads (Tadpole)".

### Fixed

- **Shared primers in a reaction:** two assays of one reaction (`mix=`) sharing their primers. The assay whose product the report gives under the other was looked for again in FASTQ input: its reads were extended, and the diagnostics called it missing. Each assay is now called on its reads, and a product the reads hold for both is reported once.
- **A 5' mismatch counted twice:** `report_no_product.tsv` counted a mismatch within a primer's first 5' bases both as a mismatch and as an unpaired 5' base.
- **Stopping a finished run:** a run stopped in the window just after it finished kept its reports, but **View Gel Image** and **View HTML Report** opened the previous run's.
- **Reports cut short:** a run whose reports could not all be written (a full disk) left them, without a QAlog, and the next run numbered its report after them. They are now removed, as for a stopped run.
- **Reads refused after running out of memory:** a FASTQ sample run again alone after running out of memory, whose reads BBDuk then refused, failed the run instead of being left out.

### Faster

- **Samples without a primer site:** a FASTQ sample in which no read holds an accepted primer site is no longer baited a second time or assembled. The second bait read all its reads to keep none: 3.6 s for 2.8 million reads with 4 threads.
- **Assemblies read once:** each assembly is read once for its sequences' descriptions, lengths and circles, instead of up to four times.
- **Unpaired reads searched once:** with unpaired reads, the baited reads are searched with blastn once instead of twice.
- **`--gel`:** the picture is drawn from the run's rows, not from `report.tsv` read back.

### Releases

A release tag is checked within seconds, before anything is built: its version, the `CHANGELOG.md` heading, and that it is on `master`. Running a release's build again no longer replaces its published ZIPs, which Zenodo and the Bioconda recipe hold. The Windows ZIP now has a `VERSIONS.txt`, as the Linux ZIP does.

### Upgrading from 2.0.0

- **`Source` column:** scripts that read `report.tsv`'s `Source` column should expect `mini-assembly` for products of FASTQ samples' contigs, where 2.0.0 wrote `assembly`. Reports written by 2.0.0 still read as they were (**Open Previous Run**, the gel).
- **Same products:** runs give the same products as with 2.0.0. With `mix=` and assays sharing their primers, `report_no_product.tsv` says which assay a product is reported under, also when the product comes from the reads.
